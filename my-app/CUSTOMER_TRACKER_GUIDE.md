# Live Booking Tracker - Customer Guide

## What is the Live Booking Tracker?

The **Live Booking Tracker** is your personal supply chain tracking system for your service booking. Just like tracking a package delivery, you can now see exactly where your service request stands in the process, from the moment you submit it to completion.

## Where to Find It

📍 **Location:** `/appointments` page  
🔍 **Look for:** The "Live Tracking" section below each booking status

## Understanding Your Journey

Your booking goes through **5 Checkpoints:**

### ✓ Checkpoint 1: Booking Submitted
**Status:** PENDING  
**What it means:** We received your request! Our team will review it shortly.  
**What to expect:** Wait for the next checkpoint within 1-2 hours

### 🔍 Checkpoint 2: Under Review  
**Status:** REVIEWED  
**What it means:** Our team is reviewing your request details.  
**What to expect:** We'll confirm the exact schedule soon and assign a mechanic

### 👨‍🔧 Checkpoint 3: Mechanic Assigned
**Status:** ASSIGNED  
**What it means:** A qualified mechanic has been assigned to your service!  
**What to expect:** You'll be contacted for final confirmation and scheduling

### ⚙️ Checkpoint 4: Service Completed
**Status:** COMPLETED  
**What it means:** Your service is done! ✨  
**What to expect:** You'll receive a summary of work done and final invoice

### 🎉 Checkpoint 5: Request Closed
**Status:** CLOSED  
**What it means:** Everything is finalized. Thanks for using Vapra Workshop!  
**What to expect:** You can rate the service and request future bookings

## Reading the Tracker

### Visual Indicators

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ✓ (Green Checkmark) | Completed checkpoint |
| ⏱️ (Spinning Clock) | Currently processing |
| ○ (Empty Circle) | Waiting checkpoint |

### Colors

- **🟢 Emerald/Green** = Completed (✓)
- **🟠 Amber/Orange** = In Progress (⏱️)
- **⚫ Gray/Slate** = Not Started (○)

### Live Indicator

- **Green pulsing dot** = Tracker is LIVE and updating
- **Text says "Live"** = Getting updates automatically

## How Often Does It Update?

✅ **Every 5 seconds** - The tracker automatically checks for status updates  
🔄 **No manual refresh needed** - Just keep the page open and watch it update  
📱 **Works on all devices** - Mobile, tablet, or desktop

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why isn't my tracker updating?
**A:** The tracker updates every 5 seconds. If it's been over 5 seconds:
- Refresh the page
- Check that you're logged in
- Close and reopen the browser tab

### Q: Can I cancel my booking once tracking starts?
**A:** 
- ✅ **CAN cancel:** While status is PENDING or REVIEWED
- ❌ **CAN'T cancel:** After ASSIGNED (contact support instead)

### Q: What if my booking gets stuck at a checkpoint?
**A:** Contact our support team if a checkpoint hasn't progressed in more than 2 hours.

### Q: Can I see old booking requests?
**A:** Yes! All your past bookings appear on the `/appointments` page with their final status.

### Q: Is my information secure?
**A:** ✅ Yes! Your tracker only shows your own bookings. Nobody else can see your status.

### Q: What if there's an error?
**A:** If you see an error message:
1. Refresh the page
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try on a different device
4. Contact support
